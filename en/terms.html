Your diary is yours

Everything you write, your moods and your photos belong to you. They're stored on your devices and in your own iCloud. We can't access them and don't use them.

Keep a backup

We work hard to keep Little Things reliable, but we can't promise it will never fail. If data is lost because of a device, iCloud or app problem, we can't recover it for you. Export a backup now and then from Me → Backup & Import → Export Backup.

Letters and moods are AI-generated

Mood guesses and the content of letters, including the patterns they point out, are generated automatically by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone and by the app. They may be inaccurate and are only for looking back. They aren't psychological, medical or any other professional advice. If you're going through a hard time, please reach out to someone you trust or to local professional services and helplines.

Little Things is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.
