Where is my diary stored?

On your iPhone, and synced through your own iCloud to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Little Things has no sign-up and no servers of its own. We can't see anyone's diary.

What happens when I get a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, turn on iCloud, install Little Things, and your diary comes back. Without iCloud, go to Me → Backup → Export Backup to save a file, then use Restore from Backup on the new phone.

Who writes the letters?

Apple Intelligence on your iPhone reads your diary and writes them on the device. Your diary is never sent anywhere. If Apple Intelligence is off or not supported, letters still arrive, with numbers and a few patterns found in your diary.

Does voice input need the internet?

When your iPhone supports it, speech is turned into text on the device. On some older models or languages, iOS may use Apple's speech recognition service instead.

Can I bring in a diary I kept in Notes?

Yes. Go to Me → Notes → Import from Notes, select all and copy the note, then come back and tap Read Clipboard. Each date needs its own line, like 11.14 or 2026.11.14.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Little Things is a diary app with no sign-up. We don't collect or sell any of your data, and we can't see what you write.

We collect no data

  • There are no accounts. You never sign up or sign in.
  • Your entries, moods, tags and settings are stored on your iPhone.
  • If your device is signed in to iCloud, your diary syncs to your own private iCloud database so it can appear on your other devices. It's kept by Apple's iCloud and can only be read by devices signed in to your Apple Account. The developer can't access it. You can turn off iCloud for Little Things in Settings anytime.
  • Your name and profile picture on the Me page (including a photo you choose or take and crop) are also stored only on your devices and in your own iCloud.
  • Little Things has no servers and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We can't see anything you write.

Permissions the app uses

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: to turn what you say into text. Used only when you tap the mic. Recordings aren't saved.
  • Face ID: if you turn on Face ID Lock, to verify it's you when you open the app. iOS handles this; Little Things never gets your face data.
  • Camera: only when you tap Take Photo to set a profile picture. The photo is cropped into a small picture; the original isn't kept.
  • Photos: choosing a profile picture uses the system photo picker. Little Things only gets the one photo you choose and can't read your photo library.
  • Notifications: if you turn on reminders, for a nightly nudge to write or to tell you a new letter has arrived.

Apple Intelligence

Letters are generated on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence. Your diary never leaves your phone.

Content you share yourself

When you use Export Backup or Sync to Notes, or copy or share entries, the content goes where you choose (like Files or Notes). This only happens when you tap to do it; after that, it's stored according to that app and your iCloud settings.

Deleting data

Deleting a day in the app also deletes it from your iCloud and other devices. Deleting the app doesn't delete the diary in iCloud; to remove it completely, go to Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete the Little Things data. We suggest exporting a backup first.

Children

Little Things doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
